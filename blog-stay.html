Choosing the right room for your family getaway.

MVD PALACE JOURNAL · STAY GUIDE

← Back to journal

A family getaway is easier to enjoy when everyone has the comfort they need. The right room is less about choosing the largest option and more about matching your group’s rhythm, ages and plans.

Start with who is travelling

Consider both the number of guests and how they will rest. Couples often value a calm, cosy layout, while families may prefer additional beds and a little more room to organise bags, snacks and bedtime routines.

Plan for the quiet hours

A great day can be undone by a difficult night. Think about early sleepers, young children and anyone who needs a little space to unwind. A family suite can make the evening feel far more relaxed when everyone does not need to follow the same schedule.

“The right room gives everyone a place to pause, so the time together feels lighter.”

Consider the reason for the trip

For a wedding or family celebration, staying close to the rest of the group can be as important as the room itself. If you are arriving for a quiet weekend, comfort and a slower pace may lead the choice instead. Tell our team what brings you here and we can guide you.

Leave room for ease

When in doubt, choose the option that makes the practical things simpler: easier bed arrangements, space for luggage, and a little margin for an unexpected guest or a long afternoon indoors.

Our Deluxe Room, Executive Room and Family Suite each offer a different balance of comfort and space. We are always happy to help you find the right fit.
